第 1 章：简介（Introduction）

1.1 目的和适用性

"The purpose of this document is to provide guidance for conducting risk assessments of federal information systems and organizations, amplifying the guidance in NIST Special Publication 800-39. This document does not prescribe a specific risk assessment methodology, but rather provides guidance for the rigorous application of risk assessment processes, methodologies, and approaches."

本文件的目的是为联邦信息系统和组织进行风险评估提供指导，放大 NIST 特别出版物 800-39 中的指导。本文档不规定特定的风险评估方法论，而是为严格应用风险评估过程、方法论和方法提供指导。

"Risk assessments are a key part of the risk management process. The guidance provided in this document can be used by federal agencies to conduct risk assessments, but the document is also useful for any organization that wants to better understand the risks it faces and the strategies it can employ to mitigate those risks."

风险评估是风险管理过程的关键部分。本文档提供的指导可供联邦机构进行风险评估使用，但该文档对任何希望更好地了解其面临的风险以及可用于缓解这些风险的策略的组织也很有用。

1.2 风险管理框架

"The risk assessment process described in this document is part of a broader risk management process, as illustrated in Figure 1. The risk management process includes: (i) framing risk; (ii) assessing risk; (iii) responding to risk; and (iv) monitoring risk."

本文档中描述的风险评估过程是更广泛的风险管理过程的一部分，如图 1 所示。风险管理过程包括：(i) 框架风险；(ii) 评估风险；(iii) 应对风险；(iv) 监控风险。

1.3 风险评估的角色

"Risk assessments are used to identify, estimate, and prioritize risks to organizational operations (i.e., mission, functions, image, and reputation), organizational assets, individuals, other organizations, and the Nation, resulting from the operation and use of federal information systems."

风险评估用于识别、估计和优先考虑组织运营（即任务、职能、形象和声誉）、组织资产、个人、其他组织和国家由于联邦信息系统的运行和使用而产生的风险。

第 2 章：基础（The Basics）

2.1 风险

"Risk is a measure of the extent to which an entity is threatened by a potential circumstance or event, and is typically a function of: (i) the adverse impacts that would arise if the circumstance or event occurs; and (ii) the likelihood of occurrence."

风险是衡量实体受到潜在情况或事件威胁的程度，通常是以下因素的函数：(i) 如果情况或事件发生将产生的不利影响；以及 (ii) 发生的可能性。

2.2 风险评估

"A risk assessment is the process of identifying, estimating, and prioritizing risks to organizational operations (i.e., mission, functions, image, and reputation), organizational assets, individuals, other organizations, and the Nation, resulting from the operation and use of federal information systems."

风险评估是识别、估计和优先考虑组织运营、组织资产、个人、其他组织和国家由于联邦信息系统的运行和使用而产生的风险的过程。

2.3 威胁、脆弱性和影响

"A threat is any circumstance or event with the potential to adversely impact organizational operations and assets, individuals, other organizations, or the Nation through an information system via unauthorized access, destruction, disclosure, modification of information, and/or denial of service."

威胁是任何具有通过信息系统对组织运营和资产、个人、其他组织或国家产生不利影响潜力的环境或事件，通过未经授权的访问、信息的破坏、披露、修改和/或拒绝服务。

"A vulnerability is a weakness in an information system that could be exploited or triggered by a threat source."

脆弱性是信息系统中可能被威胁源利用或触发的弱点。

"An adverse impact is the effect on organizational operations, organizational assets, individuals, other organizations, or the Nation of a loss of confidentiality, integrity, or availability of an information system."

不利影响是组织运营、组织资产、个人、其他组织或国家由于信息系统的机密性、完整性或可用性丧失而产生的后果。

2.4 风险 = 损失 × 概率

"Risk is a function of the likelihood of a threat event's occurrence and the resulting adverse impact. Risk assessment is the process of identifying, estimating, and prioritizing risks to organizational operations, organizational assets, individuals, other organizations, and the Nation, resulting from the operation and use of federal information systems."

风险是威胁事件发生的可能性和由此产生的不利影响的函数。风险评估是识别、估计和优先考虑组织运营、组织资产、个人、其他组织和国家由于联邦信息系统的运行和使用而产生的风险的过程。

2.5 风险容忍度

"Risk tolerance is the organization's or stakeholder's readiness to bear the risk in order to achieve its objectives. Risk tolerance may be influenced by legal or regulatory requirements (e.g., HIPAA, SOX, GLBA)."

风险容忍度是组织或利益相关者为实现其目标而承担风险的准备程度。风险容忍度可能受到法律或监管要求的影响（例如 HIPAA、SOX、GLBA）。

第 3 章：风险评估过程（The Process）

3.1 概述

"The risk assessment process is organized into three general steps: (i) prepare for risk assessment; (ii) conduct the risk assessment; and (iii) maintain the risk assessment."

风险评估过程组织为三个一般步骤：(i) 为风险评估做准备；(ii) 进行风险评估；(iii) 维护风险评估。

3.2 步骤 1：为风险评估做准备

"Prepare for the risk assessment: Identify the purpose of the assessment; Identify the scope of the assessment; Identify the assumptions and constraints; Identify the information sources; Identify the risk model and analytic approach."

为风险评估做准备：确定评估的目的；确定评估的范围；确定假设和约束；确定信息来源；确定风险模型和分析方法。

3.3 步骤 2：进行风险评估

"Conduct the risk assessment: Identify threat sources; Identify threat events; Identify vulnerabilities and predisposing conditions; Determine the likelihood of occurrence; Determine the adverse impact; Determine risk; and Communicate and share the risk assessment results."

进行风险评估：识别威胁源；识别威胁事件；识别脆弱性和诱发条件；确定发生的可能性；确定不利影响；确定风险；以及沟通和分享风险评估结果。

3.4 步骤 3：维护风险评估

"Maintain the risk assessment: Monitor risk factors; Update the risk assessment; and Communicate and share the risk assessment results."

维护风险评估：监控风险因素；更新风险评估；以及沟通和分享风险评估结果。

第 4 章：风险评估实施（Conducting Risk Assessments）

4.1 准备

"The risk assessment preparation step establishes an appropriate context for conducting the risk assessment. Key activities include identifying the purpose, scope, assumptions, and constraints; identifying information sources; and selecting a risk model, analytic approach, and assessment approach."

风险评估准备步骤为进行风险评估建立适当的上下文。关键活动包括确定目的、范围、假设和约束；确定信息来源；以及选择风险模型、分析方法和评估方法。

4.2 进行评估

"The risk assessment conduct step produces a list of risks that can be prioritized by risk level and used to inform a risk response decision. The risk level is determined by assessing the impact and likelihood of identified threats exploiting vulnerabilities with a resulting adverse impact on the system or organization."

风险评估执行步骤生成一份风险列表，可以按风险级别进行优先排序，并用于为风险应对决策提供信息。风险级别是通过评估已识别的威胁利用脆弱性对系统或组织产生不利影响的可能性和影响来确定的。

4.3 威胁源识别

"Threat sources are the intent and method targeted at the exploitation of a vulnerability or a situation and method that may accidentally exploit a vulnerability. The threat sources include: (i) hostile cyber or physical attacks; (ii) human errors of omission or commission; (iii) structural failures of organization, IT system, or equipment; and (iv) natural and man-made disasters, accidents, and failures beyond the control of the organization."

威胁源是针对漏洞利用的意图和方法，或可能意外利用漏洞的情况和方法。威胁源包括：(i) 敌对的网络或物理攻击；(ii) 人为遗漏或作为错误；(iii) 组织、IT 系统或设备的结构性故障；以及 (iv) 自然和人为灾害、事故和超出组织控制的故障。

4.4 威胁事件识别

"A threat event is an event or situation that has the potential to adversely impact organizational operations and assets, individuals, other organizations, or the Nation through an information system via unauthorized access, destruction, disclosure, modification of information, and/or denial of service."

威胁事件是有潜力通过信息系统对组织运营和资产、个人、其他组织或国家产生不利影响的事件或情况，通过未经授权的访问、信息的破坏、披露、修改和/或拒绝服务。

4.5 脆弱性识别

"Vulnerabilities and predisposing conditions are the properties of the system, its environment, or the internal controls that make the system susceptible to threats. Vulnerabilities can be found in the technical, operational, or management components of a system."

脆弱性和诱发条件是系统、其环境或内部控制使系统易受威胁影响的属性。脆弱性可以在系统的技术、运营或管理组件中找到。

4.6 可能性确定

"The likelihood of occurrence is a weighted risk factor based on an analysis of the probability that a given threat is capable of exploiting a given vulnerability, and the probability of that vulnerability being present in the system."

发生的可能性是基于对给定威胁能够利用给定脆弱性的概率以及该脆弱性存在于系统中的概率的加权风险因素。

4.7 影响确定

"The adverse impact of a threat event can be described in terms of the impact on the organization's mission, functions, image, or reputation, or the financial impact on the organization."

威胁事件的不利影响可以根据对组织的任务、职能、形象或声誉的影响，或对组织的财务影响来描述。

4.8 风险确定

"Risk is determined by assessing the likelihood that a threat event will occur and the resulting adverse impact if it does occur. Risk is a function of the likelihood of a threat event and the magnitude of the adverse impact that would result."

风险是通过评估威胁事件发生的可能性和如果发生将产生的不利影响来确定的。风险是威胁事件的可能性和将产生的不利影响大小的函数。

4.9 风险登记册

"The primary output of a risk assessment is a risk register (or risk catalog) that lists the risks identified, their impact and likelihood, the affected assets, and the recommended risk response."

风险评估的主要输出是风险登记册（或风险目录），其中列出了已识别的风险、其影响和可能性、受影响的资产以及推荐的风险应对。

第 5 章：风险沟通（Communicating and Sharing Risk Information）

5.1 风险沟通的目的

"The purpose of communicating risk information is to ensure that decision makers across the organization have the information they need to make informed risk-based decisions, and to ensure that individuals and teams with risk-related responsibilities have the information they need to fulfill their responsibilities."

沟通风险信息的目的是确保整个组织的决策者拥有做出知情风险决策所需的信息，并确保具有风险相关职责的个人和团队拥有履行其职责所需的信息。

5.2 风险沟通原则

"Effective risk communication: (i) ensures that the information communicated is appropriate to the audience; (ii) is accurate, clear, and timely; (iii) conveys the appropriate level of risk; and (iv) includes recommendations for action or options for consideration."

有效的风险沟通：(i) 确保传达的信息适合受众；(ii) 准确、清晰和及时；(iii) 传达适当的风险水平；以及 (iv) 包含建议的行动或考虑的选项。

5.3 风险分享

"Risk sharing is the practice of sharing risk among multiple stakeholders to reduce the impact of any single stakeholder's exposure to risk. Risk sharing can be accomplished through various mechanisms, including insurance, outsourcing, and partnerships."

风险分享是在多个利益相关者之间分享风险以减少任何单个利益相关者对风险的影响的做法。风险分享可以通过各种机制实现，包括保险、外包和合作伙伴关系。

第 6 章：总结（Summary）

"The risk assessment process described in this document provides a foundation for organizations to identify, estimate, and prioritize risks. By following the process described in this document, organizations can develop a comprehensive understanding of the risks they face and make informed decisions about how to respond to those risks."

本文档中描述的风险评估过程为组织识别、估计和优先考虑风险提供了基础。通过遵循本文档中描述的过程，组织可以全面了解他们面临的风险，并就如何应对这些风险做出明智的决定。

附录 A：威胁源（Appendix A: Threat Sources）

"Threat sources are categorized as: (i) hostile cyber or physical attacks; (ii) human errors of omission or commission; (iii) structural failures of organization, IT system, or equipment; and (iv) natural and man-made disasters, accidents, and failures beyond the control of the organization."

威胁源分类为：(i) 敌对的网络或物理攻击；(ii) 人为遗漏或作为错误；(iii) 组织、IT 系统或设备的结构性故障；以及 (iv) 自然和人为灾害、事故和超出组织控制的故障。

附录 B：威胁事件（Appendix B: Threat Events）

"Threat events are categorized as: (i) adversarial: hostile attacks; (ii) accidental: human errors; (iii) structural: system failures; and (iv) environmental: natural and man-made disasters."

威胁事件分类为：(i) 对抗性的：敌对攻击；(ii) 意外的：人为错误；(iii) 结构的：系统故障；以及 (iv) 环境的：自然和人为灾害。

附录 C：脆弱性（Appendix C: Vulnerabilities and Predisposing Conditions）

"Vulnerabilities are categorized as: (i) technical vulnerabilities (e.g., software defects, hardware flaws); (ii) operational vulnerabilities (e.g., procedural weaknesses, lack of training); and (iii) management vulnerabilities (e.g., inadequate policies, lack of oversight)."

脆弱性分类为：(i) 技术脆弱性（例如软件缺陷、硬件故障）；(ii) 操作脆弱性（例如程序缺陷、缺乏培训）；以及 (iii) 管理脆弱性（例如策略不足、缺乏监督）。
