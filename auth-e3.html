AI RMF 概述

"Led by the Information Technology Laboratory (ITL) AI Program, and in collaboration with the private and public sectors, NIST has developed a framework to better manage risks to individuals, organizations, and society associated with artificial intelligence (AI)."

由信息技术实验室（ITL）AI 项目领导，并与私营和公共部门合作，NIST 开发了一个框架，以更好地管理与人工智能（AI）相关的、对个人、组织和社会的风险。

"The NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF) is intended for voluntary use and to improve the ability to incorporate trustworthiness considerations into the design, development, use, and evaluation of AI products, services, and systems."

NIST AI 风险管理框架（AI RMF）旨在自愿使用，并提高在 AI 产品、服务和系统的设计、开发、使用和评估中纳入可信性考量的能力。

"Released on January 26, 2023, the Framework was developed through a consensus-driven, open, transparent, and collaborative process that included a Request for Information, several draft versions for public comments, multiple workshops, and other opportunities to provide input."

该框架于 2023 年 1 月 26 日发布，通过共识驱动、开放、透明、协作的过程开发，包括信息征集、多份公开评论草稿、多次研讨会以及其他提供意见的机会。

"It is intended to build on, align with, and support AI risk management efforts by others (Fact Sheet)."

它旨在建立于、与和支持其他人的 AI 风险管理努力（情况说明书）。

"On March 30, 2023, NIST launched the Trustworthy and Responsible AI Resource Center, which will facilitate implementation of, and international alignment with, the AI RMF. Examples of how other organizations are building on and using the AI RMF can be found via the AIRC's Use Case page."

2023 年 3 月 30 日，NIST 启动了可信和负责任 AI 资源中心，该中心将促进 AI RMF 的实施以及国际对齐。关于其他组织如何建立和使用 AI RMF 的示例可通过 AIRC 的用例页面找到。

"On July 26, 2024, NIST released NIST-AI-600-1, Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework: Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile. The profile can help organizations identify unique risks posed by generative AI and proposes actions for generative AI risk management that best aligns with their goals and priorities."

2024 年 7 月 26 日，NIST 发布了 NIST-AI-600-1《人工智能风险管理框架：生成式人工智能 Profile》。该 Profile 可以帮助组织识别生成式 AI 带来的独特风险，并提出最符合其目标和优先级的生成式 AI 风险管理行动。

"On April 7, 2026, NIST released a concept note for an AI RMF Profile on Trustworthy AI in Critical Infrastructure. The profile will guide critical infrastructure operators towards specific risk management practices to consider when engaging AI-enabled capabilities."

2026 年 4 月 7 日，NIST 发布了关于关键基础设施中可信 AI 的 AI RMF Profile 概念说明。该 Profile 将指导关键基础设施运营商在采用 AI 赋能能力时考虑具体的风险管理实践。

"To view public comments received on the previous drafts of the NIST AI Risk Management Framework and Requests for Information, see the AI RMF Development page."

要查看 NIST 风险管理框架之前草案收到的公开评论和信息征集请求，请参阅 AI RMF 开发页面。

"The AI RMF 1.0 is being revised as part of the White House AI Action Plan."

AI RMF 1.0 正在作为白宫 AI 行动计划的一部分进行修订。
