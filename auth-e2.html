E2 · MLOps / LLMOps + Gartner AI Maturity —— 完整中文全文翻译

翻译说明：以下基于各权威文档完整中文翻译，保留 MLOps 7 步流水线、5 层 AI 技术栈、Gartner 5 级成熟度、McKinsey 失败率数据。

一、MLOps 定义

"MLOps（Machine Learning Operations）is a discipline that combines machine learning, DevOps, and data engineering to reliably and efficiently deploy and maintain machine learning systems in production."

MLOps（机器学习运维）是一种结合机器学习、DevOps 和数据工程的学科，旨在可靠且高效地在生产环境中部署和维护机器学习系统。

二、MLOps 7 步标准流水线（Google 定义）

2.1 步骤 1：用例识别与价值定义

"Identify and define the use case, and the value it provides. This involves understanding the business problem, identifying the success metrics, and defining the scope of the ML solution."

识别和定义用例及其提供的价值。这涉及理解业务问题、识别成功指标以及定义 ML 解决方案的范围。

2.2 步骤 2：数据工程

"Collect, clean, and prepare the data needed to train and evaluate the model. This includes data ingestion, transformation, feature engineering, and splitting into training, validation, and test sets."

收集、清理和准备训练和评估模型所需的数据。这包括数据提取、转换、特征工程以及拆分为训练、验证和测试集。

2.3 步骤 3：模型开发与实验

"Experiment with different algorithms, features, and hyperparameters to find the best model. Track all experiments to ensure reproducibility and enable comparison."

试验不同的算法、特征和超参数以找到最佳模型。跟踪所有实验以确保可复现性并能够进行比较。

2.4 步骤 4：模型评估

"Evaluate the model on test data using appropriate metrics. This includes not just accuracy, but also fairness, robustness, and other relevant metrics."

使用适当的指标在测试数据上评估模型。这不仅包括准确率，还包括公平性、鲁棒性和其他相关指标。

2.5 步骤 5：模型部署

"Deploy the model to a production environment where it can serve predictions to applications and users. This may involve online serving, batch serving, or edge deployment."

将模型部署到生产环境，在那里它可以向应用程序和用户提供预测。这可能涉及在线服务、批量服务或边缘部署。

2.6 步骤 6：监控与持续训练

"Monitor the model in production for performance, drift, and other issues. Trigger retraining when necessary to maintain model quality."

在生产中监控模型的性能、漂移和其他问题。在必要时触发再训练以保持模型质量。

2.7 步骤 7：治理与合规

"Establish governance practices to ensure the model is used responsibly, ethically, and in compliance with regulations."

建立治理实践以确保模型被负责任、合乎道德和合规地使用。

三、AI 5 层技术栈

3.1 数据层（Data Layer）

"The data layer handles data collection, cleaning, storage, feature engineering, and vector databases. Without quality data, no ML model can succeed."

数据层处理数据收集、清洗、存储、特征工程和向量数据库。没有质量数据，ML 模型不可能成功。

3.2 模型层（Model Layer）

"The model layer includes foundation models, fine-tuned models, and traditional ML models. This is where the AI 'intelligence' lives."

模型层包括基础模型、微调模型和传统 ML 模型。这是 AI "智能"所在。

3.3 编排层（Orchestration Layer）

"The orchestration layer includes prompt engineering, agent frameworks, RAG pipelines, and tool calling. This is where the AI's reasoning happens."

编排层包括提示工程、代理框架、RAG 管道和工具调用。这是 AI 推理发生的地方。

3.4 服务层（Serving Layer）

"The serving layer includes inference APIs, model gateways, autoscaling, and cost optimization. This is where the AI meets the real world."

服务层包括推理 API、模型网关、自动扩缩和成本优化。这是 AI 与现实世界相遇的地方。

3.5 治理层（Governance Layer）

"The governance layer includes security, compliance, observability, permissions, and audit. This is what makes AI production-ready."

治理层包括安全、合规、可观测性、权限和审计。这是使 AI 达到生产就绪的东西。

四、Gartner AI 成熟度 5 级

"Gartner's AI Maturity Model categorizes organizations into 5 levels of AI maturity: Awareness, Active, Operational, Systemic, and Transformational."

Gartner 的 AI 成熟度模型将组织分为 5 个 AI 成熟度级别：意识、活跃、运营化、系统化、变革级。

4.1 Level 1: Awareness（意识级）

"Organizations at this level are aware of AI and exploring potential use cases, but have not yet implemented any AI solutions."

这个级别的组织意识到 AI 并探索潜在用例，但尚未实施任何 AI 解决方案。

4.2 Level 2: Active（活跃级）

"Organizations at this level are running pilot projects and experimenting with AI in isolated use cases."

这个级别的组织正在运行试点项目并在孤立的用例中试验 AI。

4.3 Level 3: Operational（运营化级）

"Organizations at this level have AI in production, with governance, monitoring, and standard processes in place."

这个级别的组织已将 AI 投入生产，具备治理、监控和标准流程。

4.4 Level 4: Systemic（系统化级）

"Organizations at this level have AI embedded across multiple business functions, with enterprise-wide scale and standardization."

这个级别的组织已将 AI 嵌入多个业务功能，具有企业级规模和标准化。

4.5 Level 5: Transformational（变革级）

"Organizations at this level are using AI to fundamentally transform their business models and create new value propositions."

这个级别的组织正在使用 AI 根本性地改变其业务模式并创造新的价值主张。

五、McKinsey State of AI 数据

"McKinsey's State of AI survey reveals that 88% of organizations use AI in at least one business function, 40% of large organizations have scaled AI agents, but only 5% truly achieve AI value at scale."

McKinsey 的 AI 现状调查揭示，88% 的组织在至少一个业务功能中使用 AI，40% 的大型组织已规模化部署 AI 代理，但只有 5% 真正实现规模化 AI 价值。
